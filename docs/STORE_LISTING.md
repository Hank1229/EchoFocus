## Detailed Description

EchoFocus records which sites you visit and for how long, then scores your day from 0 to 100. Your browsing history stays in your browser.

**How it works**

EchoFocus runs in the background while you browse. It records how long each site stays in front of you, sorts each site into productive, distraction, or neutral, and turns that split into a daily Focus Score (0 to 100). A pomodoro timer in the popup runs your focus rounds and breaks. If you sign in, Google Gemini reads the day's aggregated numbers at 21:00 and writes a short insight: what went well, what pulled you away, and one concrete suggestion for tomorrow.

**Where your data lives**

Every visit, URL, and page title stays in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local). Nothing leaves your device until you sign in. After that, EchoFocus syncs one aggregate per day (domain names, durations, and categories), your settings and custom rules, and the AI analysis results. It never uploads URLs, page titles, or your browsing history. From the extension's settings you can export your data or delete your tracking data at any time.

**Key features**

- **Focus Score**: a daily score from 0 to 100, based on how your productive time compares with your distraction time
- **Focus timer**: pomodoro focus rounds and breaks from the popup, with the minutes left on the extension badge
- **Daily insight and weekly review**: Gemini writes a short read of your day and your week from aggregated numbers (requires an account)
- **Web dashboard**: today's review, past days, and 7-day and 30-day trends at https://echo-focus-web.vercel.app
- **Custom rules**: move any domain into the category that fits your work
- **Export and delete**: download your data as JSON or CSV, or delete your tracking data

**Who it's for**

Developers, designers, writers, and students use it to see their browsing habits in numbers. EchoFocus keeps your browsing history on your device and sends no part of it to a third-party cloud.

**Open source**

EchoFocus is open source. You can read the code, check the privacy claims above, and contribute at:
https://github.com/Hank1229/EchoFocus
